### Speaker Notes
> Per Andrew Tran's guidance: stick to externally referenceable stories.
> HSS and Humana both have summit sessions. Premier has a published customer story.
>
> "We're seeing healthcare organizations at every stage of this journey. Some are building
> the governed data foundation. Others have moved to self-service analytics with Genie,
> where their finance teams are getting answers 10× faster. And the most advanced are
> using Genie as a true AI coworker. The common thread is that they all started with the
> same foundation: governed data, certified metrics, and a single source of truth."
